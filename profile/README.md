## What Azri does

- **FSRS scheduling.** Reviews are timed by the FSRS algorithm, so you see each card right before you'd forget it.
- **Free with no card limit.** Unlimited manual cards, decks, imports and reviews, with the full scheduler. Not a trial.
- **AI deck generation.** Turn text, photos and PDFs into flashcards in seconds, with auto-translation for vocabulary.
- **Anki import.** Bring your existing Anki decks with you.
- **Study your way.** Review sessions, quiz mode, photo, audio and vocabulary cards, and insights on your progress.
- **Everywhere you learn.** Cloud sync across iPhone and iPad, a web app, and Save to Azri for [Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/save-to-azri/ebpaleihojoaoipnhhiohonbfokcaeph) and [Safari](https://apps.apple.com/app/azri-for-safari/id6787226357).
- **Works with AI assistants.** The Azri connector lets Claude and ChatGPT create decks and cards straight into your library.

## History

Azri started in 2020 as **Simple Anki**, a lightweight Anki-style flashcard app for iOS, and grew into Azri over the following years. Read the story on the [About page](https://azri.app/about/).
